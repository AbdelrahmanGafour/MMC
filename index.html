FAQ

Quick answers.

What does MMC Group do?

MMC Group is an exhibitions and events management company based in Cairo, Egypt. We manage events, conferences and exhibitions, from small business meetings to large regional conferences.

Where is MMC Group located?

4 Mohamed Mazloum St., from Hoda Sharawi St., Bab El Louk, Cairo, Egypt.

Which services do you offer?

Sixteen services, including exhibitions and events, hotel and meeting spaces, transportation and travel arrangements, consulting, pre-event planning, budget planning and controlling, media coverage, sales and marketing, sponsorship packages, on-site logistics and support, hotel accommodation, exclusive tours and corporate gifts and awards.

Can you handle both small and large events?

Yes. We handle needs ranging from small business meetings to large regional conferences.
